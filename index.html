About

A short account of what I do and why.

I'm a 400-level Information Technology student at Osun State University, and I spend most of my time outside class writing JavaScript, shipping small tools, and trying to figure out how software can replace the paper processes still common at Nigerian institutions.

That instinct led to AttendX, an attendance platform I founded to replace manual sign-in sheets with QR codes, GPS checks, and facial verification, built with dashboards for students, lecturers, and administrators.

I also write about technology and Nigerian politics on my blog, The Nigerian Lens. My goal is to become a full-stack developer who builds for education and public institutions, and I'm currently learning and building toward that.
